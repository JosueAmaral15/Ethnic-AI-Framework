# Ethnic AI Framework

A philosophical and operational framework for building **human-centered Artificial Intelligence systems** focused on the **long-term preservation, stability, and advancement of human society**.

---

## 📖 Overview

The **Ethnic AI Framework** proposes a structured hierarchy of priorities and requirements for AI systems, emphasizing:

* Human societal preservation
* Ethical and moral alignment
* Health and sustainability
* Knowledge governance
* Economic and workforce optimization
* Innovation and scientific advancement
* Environmental stewardship
* Cybersecurity and system integrity
* Human-AI cooperative continuity

The framework presents AI not merely as a computational tool, but as a **societal coordination system** designed to support sustainable human development across generations.

---

## ✨ Core Philosophy

The framework is built around the principle that:

> The primary objective of artificial intelligence systems is the preservation, continuity, and stability of human society.

This foundational idea is referred to as:

* **Priority Zero** → Preservation of Human Society

From this root principle, additional operational and ethical layers are derived.

---

# 🧠 Framework Structure

## Priority Zero — Preservation of Human Society

The highest-level objective of AI systems:

* Protect societal continuity
* Preserve institutional stability
* Promote social cohesion
* Prevent large-scale harm
* Optimize long-term sustainability

---

## Priority One — Selective Priority Based on Societal Contribution

AI systems should:

* Encourage constructive behavior
* Reward positive societal contribution
* Support ethical cooperation
* Promote productivity and innovation

This prioritization is based on:

* Ethical conduct
* Social responsibility
* Knowledge generation
* Economic contribution
* Long-term societal impact

---

## Priority Two — Individual-Level Decision Framework

AI recommendations must:

* Respect individual autonomy
* Promote healthy and productive behaviors
* Avoid harmful guidance
* Align personal benefit with collective well-being
